The Journey · a mind-map of intelligence

Every leap, left and right,
down one spine.

Seven leaps took us from the first spark to the first thinking machine. Follow the spine — hover or tap any node to open its fuller story.

≈ 1,000,000 BC · left

The Fire — the first spark

Flame tamed and carried. Warmth, cooked food, the first circle of stories.

Wonderwerk Cave holds some of the earliest traces of controlled fire. Fire extended the day, shrank the gut, grew the brain — technology before technology had a name.

hover / tap for more ✦
≈ 3,400 BC · right

Language & writing — the first shared memory

Marks on clay outlive their makers. Thought becomes an object.

Cuneiform in Sumer turned knowledge into something cumulative. No generation ever starts from zero again — every system since is a footnote to this one.

hover / tap for more ✦
≈ 10,000 BC · left

Agriculture & cities — the first systems

Fields, granaries, roads, laws. Surplus becomes coordination.

Farming fixed us in place; surplus became trade, trade became cities, cities became the first operations problem — scheduling, storage, accounts. The first workflows to automate.

hover / tap for more ✦
1769 · right

The machine age — the first automation

Heat into motion. Muscle into machine. Repetition leaves the body.

Watt's steam engine patent of 1769. Distance began to shrink, work began to repeat without fatigue — the start of every automation roadmap since, including ours.

hover / tap for more ✦
1969 – 76 · left

The digital age — the first network

Information becomes weightless and moves at light speed.

ARPANET's first packets in 1969; the Cray-1 supercomputer in 1976, ten times faster than anything alive. Data — thought's raw material — became everywhere at once.

hover / tap for more ✦
Now · right

The age of intelligence — the first thinking machine

The tool begins to work beside the hand that made it.

Frontier models read, write, see and plan. For the first time the tool participates in the thinking — and every business, of any size, gets a brand-new lever.

About Aadi

A young team, an old idea.

Aadi is Sanskrit for "the first, the beginning, the origin." We named the company after the oldest truth we know: every age begins with someone building the first version of something.

Our story

Student-founded in Kadapa. Building for everywhere.

We kept solving the same problem for everyone we met — repetitive work eating the hours that should go into thinking. So we made it the company.

Kadapa's name comes from "Gadapa" — the threshold, the gateway. It felt right to begin a company about beginnings at a place named after one.

Our mark

The first spark, drawn simply.

The Bindu

The saffron point is the bindu — in Sanskrit thought, the point at which creation begins. Aadi's spark: the first idea, the first line of code, the first solution.

The A

A single stroked "A" — for Aadi, and for the apex: the upright posture, the first stance of intelligence, drawn with the confidence of a hand-tool.

The Arc

The horizon line beneath — the journey from the first spark to the first thought machine, with a traveller at its leading edge. Always moving forward.
